On December 5, 2025, ERCOT implemented its long-awaited market overhaul . RTC+B, short for real-time co-optimization plus batteries, was a huge step towards modernizing ERCOT’s market engines. This change was backed by cost-saving projections, better utilization of resources based on capabilities, and reduced operator intervention.
